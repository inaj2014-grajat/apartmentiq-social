Every winter the whole family fights for one patch of sun. In this house Dhoop the cat usually wins.

Then the patch moves, and everyone moves with it. How many hours of sun a flat gets in winter is something most people find out after they move in.

There is a number for it. The Sun Score is the hours of direct sun a flat gets on 21 Dec, when the days are at their shortest and the noon sun sits lowest. It is one number you can compare across flats, and two flats in the same tower can score very differently.
