"Sun-facing" is a word. A Sun Score is a number.

Every listing says a flat is bright. Few say how bright, or in which month. So two flats that sound the same can get very different sun.

The Sun Score is one number you can compare across flats: the hours of direct sun a flat gets on 21 Dec. That is when the days are at their shortest, the noon sun sits lowest and noon shadows reach furthest. Dhoop the cat judges a flat by its patch of sun. The Sun Score does the same job in hours.

ApartmentIQ traces the real sun on your exact flat, hour by hour, season by season. Free sun report during pre-launch: apartmentiq.in

The model leaves out trees, temporary structures and buildings that are not yet sanctioned. Scenes are illustrative.
