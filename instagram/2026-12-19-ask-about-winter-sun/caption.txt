The listing says sun-facing. Ask: in December too?

Most flats are shown on a bright day, and "sun-facing" rarely comes with a month. Four questions make it specific. Which way does the balcony face, by compass. Which hours does it get direct sun in December. What is the tallest building to its south. Is anything planned on the open plot next door.

If the answers are vague, check it yourself. Visit once in the morning and once in the late afternoon, and see which neighbours' balconies are in sun.
