The sun over Noida sets at 17:28 on 21 December and at 19:21 on 21 June.

It rises later in winter too: 07:08 in December against 05:23 in June. So a December day is shorter at both ends, and a flat has fewer hours in which it can catch direct sun.

That is why winter is the season to check. If a neighbouring tower shades a flat for part of a short day, little sun is left over. Visit once in the morning and once near sunset, and ask which hours the balcony gets direct sun in December.
