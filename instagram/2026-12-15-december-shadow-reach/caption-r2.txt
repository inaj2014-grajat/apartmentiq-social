Why is the December morning walk so cold? Look at the shadows. Dhoop the cat will not leave the sunny bench.

A 30 m building can throw an 85 m shadow. That is its length at 09:00 on 21 December over Noida, on flat ground. By 12:00 the same shadow is 39 m long. By 15:00 it is 64 m again, and it has swung from the north-west to the north-east.

So a building that looks far from a balcony can still shade it on a winter morning, and lower floors are usually shaded first. In December, look south-east in the morning, south at noon and south-west in the afternoon, and note anything tall.
