Visiting a flat this weekend? Four sun checks, and all you need is the compass on your phone.

A site visit shows you one hour of one day. The sun moves all day, and in December the noon sun over Noida sits low in the south, about 38° above the horizon. So in winter, what stands to the south of a balcony matters a lot.

On the visit: find south with your phone compass. Look at what stands there, how tall and how close. Find the patch of sun on the floor and note the time. Then come back at a different hour if you can.
