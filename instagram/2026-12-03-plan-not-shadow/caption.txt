A floor plan tells you where the rooms are. It doesn't tell you what shades them.

When a new tower goes up next to a flat, its shadow comes with it. In winter the sun sits lower, so that shadow reaches further, and it can cover balconies that used to get sun.

Before you buy or rent, ask what is planned on the empty plots around the building, and how tall it will be.
