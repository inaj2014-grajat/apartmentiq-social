# The Flat Hunt · series guide

An animated microseries for @apartmentiq.in. Rajat, 28 Nov 2026: "i want to create a animated microseries on this theme". Look chosen by Rajat the same day: **miniature diorama**. He approved the look test (`look_test_01_diorama.mp4`) with "do it".

Read `CONTENT_RULES.md` first. Every rule there applies to the series.

## The idea in one line

One couple hunts for a flat in Noida. In each episode they nearly fall for something, and the sun shows what the visit hid.

## The world

Everything is a handmade model on a wooden table in a dim studio.
- Towers: cream and white model apartment towers with little balconies, model trees, a model-grass lawn.
- Rooms: a cutaway "room box" of one living room, open at the front like a dollhouse, with a glass balcony door in the back wall.
- The sun: a single warm lamp. A low lamp is the winter sun. It may move in a slow arc. Its shadows are the point of every episode.
- Movement: small stop-motion gestures only (a head turns, a hand rises). No walking, no talking, no lip movement.

## The two characters

Small painted figurines. They are told apart by their coats, because faces are too small to hold from clip to clip.
- **Meera**: a long beige coat. She notices things: she is the one who looks at the window, touches the sofa, shades her eyes.
- **Kabir**: a dark coat. He looks at the price and the plan, then follows her gaze.
Names are for scripts and captions only. They are invented. Never put a surname, a builder or a society on them.

## The prompt block every clip starts from

Copy this, then add the scene:

> Generate one 8 second video. Stop-motion style macro shot of a handcrafted miniature [diorama / cutaway model of one apartment living room, open at the front like a dollhouse room box] on a wooden table in a dim studio. [Scene.] Two small painted figurines: a man in a dark coat and a woman in a long beige coat. [One small stop-motion movement.] A single warm lamp [where it is and what its light or shadow does]. Tilt-shift look, shallow depth of field, slow push-in. Sound: soft quiet room tone. No text, no captions, no logos, no music.

Flow settings: Omni 1.2 Flash, 9:16, 1 output, 24 credits a clip. No reference image is needed when the coats are named.

## The shape of every episode (about 21 seconds)

| Part | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Scene 1 | 3.5 s | The hook, on screen from the first frame. Chip: `THE FLAT HUNT · EP n` |
| Scene 2 | 4 s | The turn: what the visit did not show |
| Scene 3 | 4 s | What it can mean in winter |
| Scene 4 | 4.5 s | Always the same: the scale model under the moving lamp (`clips/model_shadow.mp4` from the 3 Dec reel). Chip `APARTMENTIQ`. "We trace the **real sun** / on your exact flat" · "hour by hour · season by season" |
| End card | 5 s | The standard end card |

Caption starts "The Flat Hunt · Episode n: <title>." and follows the caption shape in `CONTENT_RULES.md` section 5, with "Scenes are illustrative."

## What to check in every clip

1. It reads as a model: table edge or studio dark visible at some point.
2. Beige coat and dark coat are both there and not swapped.
3. The light does what the caption says. If the caption says "no sun patch", there is none.
4. No fake writing on walls, boxes or the table.
5. Flow's watermark is visible and not covered.
