## Episode 2 · Low floor, high floor · Thu 17 Dec

**The moment:** choosing a floor from the price sheet.
**One idea:** in the same tower, a low floor can sit in a neighbour's shadow in winter while a high floor clears it.
**Hook (8 words):** Low floor. High floor. Same tower. Different winter.

| Scene | Picture | Words on screen | Speech |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · 4.5 s · chip `THE FLAT HUNT · EP 2` | Winter morning in the compound. The family looks up at their tower. The tower opposite throws a shadow across the lower floors; the upper floors are lit. Dhoop sits in a strip of sun on the lawn, Chhaya in the shade. | Low floor. High floor. / Same tower. / **Different winter.** | Meera: "Low floor or high floor?" · Kabir: "The low one costs less." |
| 2 · 5 s · chip `LOW FLOOR` | Inside the low-floor flat, the same morning. The tower opposite fills the balcony door. No patch of sun on the floor. Dhoop walks to the door and looks up. Chhaya stretches out on the cool tiles. | Down here the tower opposite / **can block the low sun.** | Dhoop: "All I see is tower." · Chhaya: "Lovely and cool." |
| 3 · 5 s · chip `HIGH FLOOR` | The same room shape, high up, the same morning. The roof of the tower opposite is below the window line. A big patch of sun on the floor. Dhoop flops into it. | Up here the same sun / **can clear it.** | Dhoop: "Now we are talking." · Meera: "So that is the difference." |
| 4 · 5.5 s | The shared closing scene, unchanged. | as in `SERIES.md` | Dhoop: "Sun for me." · Chhaya: "Shade for me." |

**Caption points:** the low winter sun and a tall neighbour; "can", never "is"; ask which floors the neighbour's shadow reaches in December. No floor numbers.
**Say it no stronger:** a high floor is not always sunnier. It depends on what stands opposite and how tall it is.
**Still prompts (scene part only; wrap each in the still block from `SERIES.md`):**
1. "Outdoors on a green lawn between two tall apartment towers on a clear winter morning. The long shadow of the tower behind the camera lies across the lawn and across the lower floors of the tower in front; its upper floors are in bright sun. The woman and the man stand on the lawn seen from behind and a little to the side, looking up at the tower. The ginger tabby cat sits in a narrow strip of sunlight on the grass. The black labrador lies in the shade."
2. "An empty flat on a low floor. Through the wide balcony door a neighbouring apartment tower fills the view, very close. No direct sunlight enters the room and there is no patch of sun on the floor. The cat stands at the balcony door looking up. The dog lies stretched out on the tiles. The woman and the man stand near the front door, looking at the window."
3. Reference: the still from scene 2. "The same room on a high floor. Through the balcony door the roof of the neighbouring tower is below eye level and the sky is open. Low sunlight enters and lies in a large bright patch on the floor. The cat lies in the sun patch. The dog sits in the shade by the wall."

## Episode 3 · North-facing in December · Mon 21 Dec

**The moment:** the compass on the phone during a visit.
**One idea:** in December the sun stays in the southern sky, so a balcony facing due north gets no direct sun.
**Hook (6 words):** North-facing. No direct sun all December.
**Timely hook:** 21 December is the day the Sun Score is counted on (live site, read 28 Nov 2026).
**Needs:** a one-off publish run. The publish task runs Tue, Thu and Sat only. That is Rajat's call.

| Scene | Picture | Words on screen | Speech |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · 4.5 s · chip `THE FLAT HUNT · EP 3` | Winter noon on the balcony of a flat they are visiting. Bright blue sky, the balcony floor in even shade, no sun on it. Meera holds her phone flat in her palm, back of the phone to the camera. Dhoop walks the length of the balcony looking for sun. | North-facing. / **No direct sun** / all December. | Meera: "The compass says north." · Dhoop: "And where is the sun?" |
| 2 · 5 s · chip `IN DECEMBER` | From the balcony, looking across at the next tower: its south face glows in low sun. Gutar the pigeon sits on a sunny railing over there. Dhoop stares across from the shade; Chhaya lies content. | In December the sun stays / in the **southern sky.** | Dhoop: "The pigeon has my sun." · Chhaya: "Good for the pigeon." |
| 3 · 5 s · chip `ALL DAY` | Their living room in that flat, a winter afternoon. Bright window, soft even light, no sun patch anywhere. Meera in a shawl. Dhoop sits in the middle of the floor, facing the window. | Bright sky. / **No sun patch.** All day. | Meera: "Still looking, Dhoop?" · Dhoop: "Since morning." |

**Facts for the caption (run and source them at build time; values printed on 28 Nov 2026):** on 21 Dec over Noida the sun is in the south-east at 07:30 (`--time 07:30`, `sun_direction`), due south at solar noon (`--time 12:18`) and in the south-west at 17:00 (`--time 17:00`). On 21 Jun it is in the north-east at 06:00.
**Say it no stronger:** this holds for a balcony facing due north, on flat ground. Say "due north" in the sub-line or the caption. A balcony facing north-east or north-west can catch some early or late sun.
**Still prompts (scene part only):**
1. "A balcony high up on an apartment tower at noon in winter. Clear blue sky. The balcony floor and the wall are in even shade; no direct sunlight falls on the balcony. Other towers in the distance are sunlit. The woman stands holding a phone flat on her open palm, its plain back towards the camera. The man looks over her shoulder. The cat walks along the balcony floor. The dog sits. No screen is visible."
2. "View from a shaded balcony towards a neighbouring apartment tower whose facade glows in low golden winter sunlight. One plump grey pigeon sits on a sunlit railing of that tower. In the foreground, in shade, the cat sits on the balcony floor staring across at the pigeon and the dog lies relaxed."
3. "Their furnished living room on a winter afternoon. The window and balcony door are bright with soft even daylight. No direct sunlight enters the room and there is no patch of sun on the floor. The woman sits on the sofa in a shawl. The cat sits upright in the middle of the floor facing the window. The dog sleeps."

## Episode 5 · Where the neighbours dry their clothes · Thu 31 Dec

**The moment:** standing in the compound on a visit, looking up.
**One idea:** the side where people dry their clothes is often the side that gets the sun.
**Hook (7 words):** Look where the neighbours dry their clothes.

| Scene | Picture | Words on screen | Speech |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · 4.5 s · chip `THE FLAT HUNT · EP 5` | In the compound, looking up at one face of a tower in sun: clothes hang on balcony after balcony. Meera points up. Dhoop looks up too. | Look where / the neighbours / **dry their clothes.** | Meera: "Look at all the washing." · Kabir: "Only on this side." |
| 2 · 5 s · chip `THE OTHER SIDE` | Round the corner: the other face, in shade. Bare balconies. Drying racks pushed indoors against the windows. Chhaya is happy here; Dhoop is not. | The people who live here / **already know.** | Chhaya: "This side is nicer." · Dhoop: "This side is wrong." |
| 3 · 5 s · chip `THE SUNNY SIDE` | Their own balcony in the sun. Washing moves on the line. Dhoop sleeps in the sun; Chhaya sleeps in the shade of a hanging sheet. | The sunny side / often **shows itself.** | Dhoop: "Sun for me." · Chhaya: "Shade for me." |
| 4 · 5.5 s | The shared closing scene, unchanged. | as in `SERIES.md` | as in episode 1 |

**Say it no stronger:** "often", never "always". Some flats have one balcony only, and some people dry clothes indoors.
**Check every still:** no writing or logos on the clothes. Plain colours only.
**Still prompts (scene part only):**
1. "Outdoors at the foot of a tall apartment tower on a sunny winter day, looking up along its sunlit face. Plain coloured clothes hang drying on many of the balconies. The woman points up at them; the man looks up. The cat sits beside them looking up. The dog sits. No writing or logos on any clothes."
2. "Outdoors at the foot of the same tower, on its shaded side. The face of the tower is in shade. The balconies are bare; a few folded drying racks stand indoors behind the glass. The dog lies happily on the cool paving. The cat sits stiffly with its back half turned. The woman and the man look up."
3. "Their own balcony in bright sunlight. Plain coloured clothes and one white sheet move gently on a clothes line. The cat sleeps in the sun on the balcony floor. The dog sleeps in the shade cast by the hanging sheet. One plump grey pigeon on the railing. No writing or logos on any clothes."
