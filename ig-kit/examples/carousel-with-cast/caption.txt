The December sun over Noida climbs only 38° at noon. In June it is almost overhead.

A low sun means long shadows. At noon on 21 Dec, a 100 m tower throws a shadow about 128 m long. On 21 Jun the same tower's shadow is about 9 m.

So a flat that is bright on a summer site visit can sit in a neighbour's shadow in winter. Ask what stands to the south, and how tall it is.
